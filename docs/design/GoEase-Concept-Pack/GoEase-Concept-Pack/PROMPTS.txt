GOEASE — IMAGE GENERATION PROMPTS

Tool: built-in image_gen. No fallback CLI used.
Primary references: reference/goe-original.png; 01-chao-mung-trang-chu.png as the style anchor after the first generation.
Finals are selected and visually reviewed concept boards; prices, people, and maps are illustrative.

BOARD 01 — 01-chao-mung-trang-chu.png

Use case: ui-mockup.
Project: GoEase, a Vietnam travel planning PWA, eventual native mobile app. Create a professional product design concept image, not a marketing landing page.
BRAND SYSTEM: warm ivory #FBF7EE background, deep forest green #184C43 primary and text, muted sage #E7EDE2 secondary surfaces, honey gold #E7BA70 buttons, subtle terracotta #C78163 accents, warm gray #7F857B body text. These colors match the user's existing GoEase landing page. Large elegant literary serif headings (like Cormorant Garamond), clean Vietnamese-capable sans serif (like Be Vietnam Pro) for UI. Rounded cards 20px, very fine warm borders, understated shadows, thin outline icons, large readable type, generous spacing, travel magazine atmosphere, real Vietnam destination photography, muted warm natural light. NO purple neon, NO dominant blue UI.
REFERENCE IMAGE ROLE: the attached blue 3D bird is GoEase's mascot Goe. Preserve its recognizable blue feathers, cream heart-shaped face, large navy eyes, yellow beak and feet, travel scarf with white route marks, blue satchel. Keep it BLUE. Do not redesign its identity. It is a small warm companion within the calm brand, never fills information-heavy screens.
BOARD FORMAT: high-resolution landscape 2560 x 1600. Clean cream presentation canvas with GoEase wordmark at upper left, small board number and title, restrained editorial layout. Display TWO large perfectly upright flat mobile UI artboards side-by-side, both with the same 390x844 aspect ratio, thin sage border and soft radius, no heavy physical phones, no perspective distortion. Both screens must be fully visible and unclipped including bottom navigation and safe area. Labels outside artboards are small and neat. Spend most of the image area on legible UI, minimal external explanation. Mobile views are installed PWAs with no browser address bar. Small 9:41 OS status bar is allowed. Consistent persistent bottom navigation on main screens: "Khám phá", "Chuyến đi", "Goe", "Đã lưu", with simple outline compass, itinerary, mascot/spark, bookmark icons. Active state forest green.
Language: accurately accented Vietnamese. Exact GoEase capitalization. Show only the specified concise copy. Avoid tiny paragraphs, illegible filler text and excessive labels.
All destination names, costs, photos and community examples are illustrative. Never imply an operational product or real availability; small footer on the BOARD reads "GOEASE · PRODUCT CONCEPT · DỮ LIỆU MINH HỌA". Do not put developer or implementation notes within app UI.
